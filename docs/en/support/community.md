# Community & Commercial Support

This page summarizes Kuboard's free community channels and commercial support (for paid users), so you can quickly find the right way to get help.

## Free Support

| Channel | Description | For |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **WeChat / QQ community** | Q&A and best-practice sharing | All users |
| **GitHub** | Submit issues, report problems, or star the project in [kuboard-press](https://github.com/eip-work/kuboard-press) | All users |
| **Email subscription** | Be the first to hear about releases and product news | All users |

The WeChat / QQ community and subscription entries are available on the [Kuboard License & Support](./index) page.

## Subscribe to Release Updates

Fill in your subscription info at the bottom of the [Kuboard License & Support](./index) page to stay up to date with Kuboard releases and product news.

## Commercial Support (Enhanced Edition)

| Channel | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Dedicated support channel for paid users** | A dedicated channel with faster, more direct responses |
| **Remote assistance** | Official staff troubleshoot and resolve issues remotely when needed |

For the full feature list and licensing options of the enhanced edition, see [License Installation](./license-install).

## Getting a License

Commercial support and additional cluster capacity are unlocked with a Kuboard License:

- The free edition can manage up to 3 Kubernetes clusters for free;
- To manage more clusters or get commercial support, purchase a [License file](./license-install) and import it to activate.
